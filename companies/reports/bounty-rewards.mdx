A bounty program uses a table of fixed amounts for two target tiers and four severities: **Critical**, **High**, **Medium**, and **Low**. The target's tier and the report's final severity guide the primary bounty. Each target also has its own **Bounty** eligibility setting.

## Manage the bounty table

Open your program's **Manage** page and find **Bounty table**. A program **SuperAdmin** can edit the eight amounts and click **Save bounty table**. Hackrate emails other program administrators when an amount changes. The table uses the program's currency.

A **SuperAdmin** can also use **Enable bounties** and **Save bounty status** to turn bounty rewards on or off. The existing table is preserved while bounties are off. A program needs a configured bounty table before bounties can be enabled.

<Note>
  Update the table and target eligibility before inviting researchers to a new reward program. Researchers can review the published table and eligible targets on the program page.
</Note>

## Award a primary bounty

Open the report and confirm its status, severity, target tier, and bounty eligibility. In **Administration**, click **Give bounty**, enter the amount, and submit it. The report records the primary award and the researcher receives a reward notification.

The action appears for **Accepted**, **Resolved**, or **Duplicate** reports when the program has bounties enabled, the reporter has a registered account, and the report has no primary bounty yet. A known issue cannot receive a bounty.

## Add a bonus

Use **Give bonus** in the report's **Administration** card to record an additional reward. The bonus is tracked separately from the primary bounty. The action is available only on eligible reports that have not already received a bonus.

## Review spending

Open your organization's [Bounty ledger](/companies/reports/bounty-ledger) to review monthly awards, bonus totals, and reports that still need a primary bounty. You can filter the ledger by year, program, target, reward state, status, and severity, then click **Export CSV**.

If your organization has programs in more than one currency, review each currency total separately.
