Open **Analytics** from a program or organization to review report volume, triage progress, target risk, and reward spending. The organization view compares accessible programs; the program view focuses on one engagement.

## Program analytics

Choose **All time**, **30 days**, **90 days**, **12 months**, or **This year**. The page shows severity distribution, report lifecycle, target risk concentration, and response and resolution measures.

For bounty programs, **Reward & budget intelligence** shows awarded rewards, projected rewards, the configured budget, and the Hackrate fee. Click **Cost control** to inspect total commitment, remaining budget, and projected exposure. The fee counts toward budget usage, and the page flags projected spending that approaches or exceeds the budget.

## Organization analytics

Use the organization filters to select programs and a time period. Compare program resolution, open high-impact risk, researchers, rewards, and target performance. Click **View bounties** to open the [organization bounty ledger](/companies/reports/bounty-ledger).

When selected programs use different currencies, organization reward totals are shown by currency. A combined reward trend is hidden for mixed currencies.

## Report statistics visibility

If you are not a **SuperAdmin**, the **Show all report statistics** switch can include every report status in aggregate metrics. Turning it on does not change which report lists or individual reports you can open. Leave it off to use only the statuses available in your standard report view.
