---
title: "Review the organization bounty ledger"
sidebarTitle: "Bounty ledger"
description: "Track bounty and bonus awards, find reports that need a primary bounty, and export the ledger."
---

Open your organization and select **Bounties** to review rewards across its programs. The ledger shows the current **Needs bounty** queue above monthly groups of awarded reports. You can see primary bounties, bonuses, total rewards, and the number of awarded reports for the selected year.

Use **Award year**, **Programs**, **Targets**, **Reward state**, **Report status**, and **Severity** to narrow the view. Search for a report ID, program, target, or researcher. Click **Export CSV** to download the filtered ledger.

The table includes the report, program, bounty, bonus, total reward, award date, target, researcher, severity, status, and reward state. Amounts remain in each program's currency, so review currency totals separately when your organization uses both USD and EUR.

The **Analytics** role can inspect the ledger but cannot open report management actions. Administrators with report access can open an eligible report from the ledger to award its bounty.

For payout setup and eligibility, see [Bounty rewards](/companies/reports/bounty-rewards).
