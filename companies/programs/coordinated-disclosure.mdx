Coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD) is the process of handling a specific security finding with the researcher and any other affected parties. Your [vulnerability disclosure policy](/companies/programs/vulnerability-disclosure) sets the reporting rules and channel. CVD turns a report into an agreed response plan.

## Work through a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive and acknowledge">
    Give the researcher a report reference, a contact, and a date for the next update. Ask for affected products or versions, reproduction steps, and evidence when needed. Keep sensitive details in the agreed reporting channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate and prioritize">
    Check whether the finding is in scope, reproduce it where authorized, and assess its impact. Record missing evidence and possible duplicates. Route urgent findings to the right response owner. Use the [report triage guide](/companies/reports/triaging) to track the finding in Hackrate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree on a coordination plan">
    Identify who will fix the issue, who will update the researcher, and who can approve a public release. Agree on an update cadence and a proposed disclosure date. Include affected vendors or downstream users when they need to act.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remediate and verify">
    Implement a fix or mitigation and check affected versions. Agree on any researcher retesting before it starts. Record what was verified and what remains open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coordinate publication">
    Align the fix, advisory, affected-version details, user notifications, and researcher credit. If a CVE is appropriate, coordinate its assignment with the relevant CNA. An eligible resolved report can be submitted for [Hacktivity publication](/researchers/rewards/hacktivity). A program SuperAdmin reviews the submission before publication.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Set acknowledgement, assessment, remediation, and disclosure dates that your team can meet. If the risk or fix plan changes, tell the researcher what changed and when to expect the next update. Remediation and public disclosure can have different dates.

Hackrate can support intake, validation, and researcher communication under your managed VDP. Your organization owns remediation, risk decisions, and release approval. See Hackrate's [public CVD guide](https://www.hckrt.com/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure) for a fuller process overview.
