Save your bank transfer details before you expect a bounty payout. Check them carefully so Hackrate can process your reward using the correct account.

## Open payment settings

Sign in as a researcher, open **Account Settings**, and select **Payment**. The page shows your active payout method and a form for your payout preferences.

## Add bank transfer details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your country of residence">
    Select your current **Country of Residence**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who will receive the payout">
    If the reward should go to your company bank account, select **I am representing as a Company**. Otherwise, leave it unselected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your bank account">
    Select **Bank transfer** and provide:

    - **Bank Account Number (IBAN)**
    - **Bank Account Holder Name**
    - **Bank Account Country**
    - **SWIFT / BIC Code**

    Use the legal name of the person or company that owns the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    Check the account number, account holder, country, and SWIFT or BIC code. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Incorrect bank details can delay or misdirect a transfer. Review them before saving and update them if your bank account changes.
</Warning>

## Complete identity verification

You must complete [identity verification](/researchers/account/verification) before Hackrate can process your first bounty payout. You can save bank details before verification is complete.

## Check the program currency

The program brief shows whether bounties are denominated in USD or EUR. If your bank account uses another currency, ask your bank about conversion rates and transfer fees.

## Keep your details current

Return to **Account Settings → Payment** and update your information if you change bank accounts, move to another country, or start receiving payouts through a company.

If a transfer fails or you need help with a payout, contact Hackrate support with your corrected details. Do not send your password or full bank account number in an email.
