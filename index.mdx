Whether you're a company launching a bug bounty program or a researcher hunting for your next finding, this documentation covers everything you need to get started and succeed on the platform.

## What is Hackrate?

Hackrate provides four core services:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Managed Bug Bounty" icon="bug" href="/companies/programs/bug-bounty">
    Crowdsource vulnerability discovery with a global community of ethical hackers and pay only for valid, verified findings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vulnerability Disclosure (VDP)" icon="flag" href="/companies/programs/vulnerability-disclosure">
    Give researchers a safe, structured channel to report vulnerabilities — even without a cash bounty.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pentest as a Service" icon="terminal" href="/companies/programs/ptaas">
    Engage verified security professionals for targeted, time-boxed penetration testing projects.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug" href="/companies/integrations/github">
    Connect Hackrate to GitHub, Jira, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Zapier to fit your existing workflow.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
